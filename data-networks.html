WiFi and home data networks

Reliable, strong WiFi throughout your home

Your WiFi and data network is your connection to the outside world. We design and install wired networks and WiFi that cover everywhere in the house. No more dropped WhatsApp or FaceTime calls.

Talk to us 01737 448049

An essential utility

Your connection to the outside world

Alongside your water, gas and electricity supply, your internet connection is now seen as an essential utility. From your incoming fibre broadband, to your WiFi and down to your iPhone or the family's Nintendo Switch, we make sure you're connected and stay that way.

It goes deeper than that, though. Many devices in the modern home need a WiFi connection to work properly, and the number of networked devices grows vastly in a smart home. They all use the data network to talk to, and at times control, one another. There's no sign of this slowing, with video doorbells, security cameras, smart plugs and even smart washing machines (more in our post What is a smart home?).

Home working and video meetings put even more demand on home networks. We all know how frustrating it is when a work Zoom call cuts out or a file takes forever to download.

Wired access points placed from a heat-map survey: strong signal in every room

How we get it right

WiFi that just works, and lets you forget about it

Heat-map planning

Before anything goes on the wall, we survey and predict coverage with our WiFi heat-mapping tool, so every access point goes in the best position.

Wired where it matters

For the best WiFi, each access point gets its own data cable back to the main router. Fewer boxes, more coverage, more speed.

Ready for home working

Video calls that don't drop, files that download quickly, and room for every device in the house asking for a piece of the pie.

Renowned manufacturers

We carefully select products from manufacturers like Cisco, Draytek and Ruckus, all of which live up to the demands of the modern home.

The backbone of your home

Your data network is the backbone of any home, especially a smart home

That's why we encourage our clients to invest in a reliable, well-performing data network. It makes all the devices happier, even down to the kids' Nintendo Switch.
