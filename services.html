How we do it

The same simple process, every time

  1. 01

    Design

    We help you with the technology design of your home: from wiring, to interiors, to how it's going to work. Collaboration is key. We listen, advise and work with you and your team.

    • Cabling for today and tomorrow
    • Schematic drawings showing where cables should go
    • Layout drawings for sockets and equipment
    • Elevation and profile drawings for recessed TVs and home cinemas
    • Heat-map planning for WiFi coverage
  2. 02

    Installation

    We work with you, your builders and electricians to install. Kind, courteous and considerate. It's your home, and we treat it that way.

    • Terminate and test the cables we'll be using
    • Install built-in equipment like in-ceiling speakers, media sockets, lighting keypads and touch screens
    • Mount final-fit items like amplifiers, TVs, blinds and WiFi points
    • Commission everything to work as we agreed with you at the start
  3. 03

    Delivery

    We hand over and walk through everything with you. And we leave you with a clear and simple instruction book in case you forget anything, or something goes wrong.

    • A walk round the house, testing the systems together
    • Everything explained in plain English
    • A simple handbook of practical fixes to get you back up and running, quickly
