Home automation systems

Control your home with a simple-to-use system

Part of our job is to make your home simple to use, so you want to use and enjoy it. For the record, we still believe wholeheartedly in the power of a simple on-off switch.

Talk to us 01737 448049

Simplicity

Remote controls… there are too many of them

Put them side by side and you quickly realise it's a multi-step process just to turn the TV on. At Mesh we strongly believe in simplicity. That's why we design and install easy-to-use control systems that take the guesswork out of controlling what's in your home.

A couple of presses of a button and you'll be enjoying your film, without the hassle or guesswork of setting every part of your system to the right setting.

Buttons and screens

Sometimes buttons are better than screens

Other times, screens are better than buttons. So we design systems that give you both.

Buttons you can feel

We like buttons, ones you can touch and feel. Control your TV from a remote you don't even need to look at.

Your phone, when it suits

The same TV can be controlled from an app on your phone, the device that's most likely to be with you most of the time.

One app for the whole home

We love apps, but there are often too many. We integrate your other devices, so you have one app to control your home, even when you're not there.

For the record

We still believe in the simple on-off switch

Part of our job is to make your home simple to use, so you want to use and enjoy it. Clever technology should never get in the way of turning the lights on.
