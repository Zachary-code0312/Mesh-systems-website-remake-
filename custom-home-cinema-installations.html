Home cinema

Custom home cinemas and media rooms, designed and installed

Indulge in a touch of luxury with your own home cinema. Films, box sets, concerts, sports and video games as they're meant to be viewed, without random strangers munching their popcorn and nachos in your ear!

Talk to us 01737 448049

Your own cinema

It never stops being an event

Your own cinema is special, and you can design it exactly as you want it. You get to sit in the sweet spot every time. You get the best sound, so speech is clear and understandable while the special effects lift everything to the level of a proper event. It never stops being a reason to have friends round.

Really great home cinema is about creating the right level of immersion in the space you have available.

Getting it right

It's all about balance

It's worth remembering that more isn't always better.

The right size screen

Depending on the room, we design and install home cinemas with a projector or a large TV, sized to the space and where you sit.

The right level of audio

For the best surround sound, we often design systems on the latest Dolby Atmos, for sound above you as well as around you.

The right room

The right compensation for the light levels and acoustics of the room, so everything looks and sounds as it should.

Acoustics

Getting balanced sound is a real skill

It takes careful thought about the acoustics of the space, so the whole sound spectrum is at the right level. We'll work to understand the room shape, inform construction and materials for sound absorption and reflection, and recommend speaker quantities and sizes.

Investment in room design and acoustic treatment will pay dividends with any specification of audio equipment.

What we listen for

  • BassPowerful, but not overwhelming and boomy
  • SpeechClear and audible
  • High endCrisp, but not sharp

Sound all around

Sound above you, as well as around you

One under-appreciated benefit of Dolby Atmos is how much it improves the sound if you're not sat right in the middle.

We've designed rooms small to large using a number of respected brands, including Bowers & Wilkins, Anthem, Monitor Audio, Denon and Arcam, among others.

And we can provide the ultimate finishing touches, like cinema seating, and blackout blinds and curtains.
