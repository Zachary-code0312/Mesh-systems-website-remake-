TVs and audio systems

Music, TVs and home cinema

Long gone are the days of four TV channels and watching whatever happens to be on. With Netflix, Amazon Prime Video and Apple TV+, there's a raft of entertainment at your fingertips. We make it effortless to enjoy.

Home entertainment

From a good experience to a great one

At Mesh, we turn what is a good experience (thanks Netflix) into a great experience, with carefully selected home entertainment systems.

4K, 8K, HDR, surround sound, Dolby Atmos, Dolby Vision: the list of the latest technologies can be confusing (we'll leave 3D glasses in the past!). We take care of the techno-jargon and explain what matters in plain English, so you can decide what's worth having. Then you can sit back and enjoy the latest film or binge-worthy box set.

Music and TV

Access everything, everywhere

The expectation now is to watch and listen to whatever you want, whenever you want, wherever you are in the house.

Every TV in the house

We design systems that let you watch your favourite programmes and films on any television in the house.

Multi-room music

With a multi-room audio system from Sonos, a simple phone app lets you choose what to listen to, in which room. Guests coming over? Join all the rooms together.

Speakers to suit the room

A free-standing wireless speaker in the corner, a pair of elegant floor-standing speakers, or in-ceiling speakers tucked away.

Dinner party to house party

With the right speakers and amplifiers, a system that takes you from background music over dinner to rocking the house when the party starts.

Home cinema

Want the big-screen experience?

We also design and install home cinemas that bring the big screen to your home. A large screen, a projector and carefully placed speakers to really immerse you in what's happening. Do it right, and some even say it's better than going to an actual cinema.
