PROJECT 01

Weybridge House WiFi Upgrade

Brief: reliable WiFi.

What we did

  • WiFi and home network

Brands we liked

  • Ruckus
  • Draytek
  • Specialist Cables

Background

Despite having a great fibre internet connection to the house, the client had long-running problems with their WiFi, resigning themselves to 4G, or regularly "switching off and switching on again" to get it working even for a day.

Over time, they'd tried a number of ways of spreading WiFi through the house, adding powerline adaptors (internet over your electrical cables) and a "meshed" WiFi system.

Powerline adaptors can work well, but only in specific circumstances. And they typically don't work together, so the more you add, the more competing WiFi networks you have. Eventually this starts to impair their performance and defeats the object! Adding a separate meshed WiFi system just added to the congestion.

Our solution

We knew we needed far fewer WiFi boxes, while offering as much coverage and speed as possible. We also knew that, for the best WiFi, each box should have its own data cable back to the main internet router.

With only one usable network cable in the house to start with (on the ground floor, from one side of the house to the other), we hid another cable to the loft behind the drainpipe, so we could fit WiFi units in the loft to cover the first floor.

Before choosing the final position of the upstairs WiFi boxes, we tested and surveyed the coverage from each one with our WiFi heat-mapping tool, so we could clearly see the best positions.

We also swapped the BT router for a Draytek one, for more reliability (and better suited to the busy family life of working, studying and playing from home), and added two small network switches for devices that benefit from a wired connection.

We reconnected and tested everything (TVs, printers, laptops and so on) and checked the streaming services, like Netflix, iPlayer and Amazon Prime, before walking through the system with the client and handing over the user guide.

The result: great WiFi for the house and the annex, which gave the client their first trouble-free weekend in a long time.

PROJECT 02

Large Country Home

The latest in home technology for a large Surrey family home.

What we did

  • Home cinema
  • Lighting
  • Blinds
  • WiFi and home network
  • Home control system
  • Multi-room audio
  • Multi-room video

Brands we liked

  • Lutron
  • Bowers & Wilkins
  • Control4
  • Sonos
  • Sony
  • Ruckus

In this large Surrey home, the family wanted the latest in home technology, both to help them enjoy the home and to simplify how they lived in it.

Lighting and blinds

Top of the list was lighting control, so the family could have all the lighting effects they wanted, and also easily turn the house on or off as they came and went. We used Lutron, with custom light switch faceplates from Wandsworth, in aged brass or stainless steel to match the interior plans. We worked with the family so the switches had the same set of understandable controls in every room, making it easy to remember which button to press, whether the switch had 2 or 4 buttons.

We also worked with the interior designers to provide Lutron sheer and blackout blinds in the main living rooms, with fabrics chosen from Lutron's wide range. These are controlled from the same custom switches.

Finally, we added the Lutron app so the lights can be controlled from a phone, anywhere, and set dawn and dusk timers for the external lights, both for security and for the garden effects.

Home cinema

For the audio-visual experience, we installed a dedicated home cinema with the latest ultra-high-definition laser projector, cinema seating, and Dolby Atmos sound from B&W (four speakers around the sides, four in the ceiling, two subwoofers, plus the usual front left, centre and right) to create a truly immersive "this is better than Odeon" experience. The client wanted to use it for sport (especially football and American football) as well as gaming and films. We fitted Apple TVs and set up their Curzon Home Cinema app.

The system is easily controlled from a Control4 remote, with one or two buttons to press for the whole system to be ready. All the family had to do was bring the popcorn!

TV, audio and WiFi

For the rest of the house, we fitted a mix of 55-inch and 65-inch Sony OLED TVs, connected to Sky Q and Apple TVs, plus games consoles in the kids' bedrooms. The family had five Sky Q boxes, with one shared between the kitchen and dining room.

For audio, we fitted B&W ceiling speakers in ten rooms, with Sonos Amps. The client also had a vast music library on a computer hard drive, so we connected that to the Sonos system too.

Last, but by no means least, we fitted a whole-home WiFi network from Ruckus, including the large rear garden, with external weatherproof WiFi boxes. We carried out a predictive heat-map survey of the property to plan the best WiFi locations.

The result: every room in the house has great signal, and the client can walk from top to bottom without dropping off the WiFi or losing a call.

PROJECT 03

South-West London Home

A refurbishment of the ground and first floors.

What we did

  • WiFi and home network
  • Media room
  • Lighting control

Brands we liked

  • Lutron
  • Ruckus

A refurbishment project for a family who revamped their ground and first floors.

Lighting control

We worked with the interior designers to specify a Lutron lighting control system for their selection of light fittings, controlled by Lutron keypads and the Lutron app. We configured the scenes during an evening with the designers and the client, once the furniture was all in place, to get just the right levels. And the Lutron app makes it easy for our client to make adjustments without needing to call us back each time.

Media room

In the media room, we fitted a Dolby Atmos system (speakers around the sides and overhead) for the ultimate audio experience, with the speakers hidden in the ceiling and walls to keep the installation amazingly clean. The TV itself rises up from a custom-built piece of joinery, so the living room works as a peaceful relaxation room, or a jump-out-of-your-seat "Friday night is film night" experience, without the TV and speakers dominating the room.

WiFi

Lastly, as with most of our projects, we fitted a whole-house WiFi system and fast home network, using Ruckus as the brand we know will work for years.

PROJECT 04

London Duplex Apartment

Blinds, a media room and audio for a two-floor apartment.

What we did

  • Vertical blinds
  • Media room: OLED TV, Apple TV
  • Multi-room audio
  • WiFi and home network

Brands we liked

  • Bowers & Wilkins
  • Future Automation
  • Sonos
  • Ruckus

The electrics and motors for the curtains and blinds were already installed when the client bought the apartment, so we worked with them to choose the fabric and style of blinds, then added them into the apartment's iPad controls.

We fitted a 55-inch Panasonic OLED TV into the custom-built recess, on a Future Automation bracket that pulls out and swivels the TV for viewing from either the living or the dining area. We connected an Apple TV and PlayStation installed in the cupboard below. The TV is controlled from an all-in-one Control4 remote, for ease of use.

Four areas of the apartment were fitted with B&W ceiling speakers, linked to Sonos Amps in the equipment cupboard.

The result: a small WiFi system gives whole-apartment coverage, solving the bad signal between upstairs and downstairs.

PROJECT 05

Small Country Home

A family's first home outside London.

What we did

  • WiFi and home network
  • Multi-room audio

Brands we liked

  • Sonos
  • Bowers & Wilkins

This was the family's first home outside London. They weren't sure how much time they'd be spending in the house, so didn't want to install too much when they first moved in, in case they decided to let it out for much of the time.

We worked with them to install a whole-home WiFi system covering the two floors and roughly 3,000 sq ft, and three rooms of Sonos audio, with either bookshelf or floor-standing speakers from B&W.
