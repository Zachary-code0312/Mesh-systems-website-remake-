Your windows, your way

How do you want to control your blinds?

That's the question we ask. There's nothing like the luxury of silent, remote-controlled blinds. You can choose from any style, including roller, roman, venetian, skylight and honeycomb, as well as automating curtains.

With the right fabrics, window treatments can also play an important role in heating your home, and in preventing it over-heating, helping you save energy.

Why go electric

Electric blinds have a number of advantages

More convenient

No more walking around the room, or the house, to raise or lower every blind, twice a day.

Hard-to-reach windows

They're often the only option for skylights and tall or awkward windows.

Safer for small children

Electric blinds don't need loose cords.

Automatic

From a simple "open or close at a given time", to wooden blinds that tilt their slats according to where the sun is in the sky.

Perfectly aligned

Built-in hem alignment, so when you move them as a group, they're all perfectly aligned and stay that way.

Control from anywhere

From your bed, from the sofa, or when you're not at home at all.

Simpler installation

We're big fans of battery-operated blinds

Technology has improved so much that, apart from changing your batteries every 3 to 5 years (they are indeed standard batteries, and they do indeed last that long), battery-operated blinds are no different to mains-powered blinds. Except they're a lot simpler to install.

3–5 yrs

Typical battery life, using regular batteries you can buy anywhere.
