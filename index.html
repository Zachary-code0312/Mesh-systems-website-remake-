What we do

Everything your home needs, working together

From the WiFi that underpins it all to the switch on the wall, we design and install systems that are reliable, enjoyable and simple to use.

WiFi and data networks

Now at the heart of almost everything, and the most frustrating thing if it doesn't work. We design and install wired networks and WiFi that just work, cover everywhere in the house, and let you forget about it. No more dropped WhatsApp or FaceTime calls.

Find out more

Lighting control

Lighting makes your home, home. We provide lighting control systems that are energy efficient and let you create your mood, but can still be turned on and off with a simple switch.

Find out more

AV entertainment systems

Understandably, often the most important thing! The right balance of big and little picture, big and little sound, wherever you want it: from a small study TV to free-standing or invisible in-ceiling speakers.

Find out more

Home cinema

Indulge in a touch of luxury with your own dedicated cinema. The latest movies, box sets, concerts, sports or video games as they're meant to be viewed, with high definition pictures and sound.

Find out more

Electric blinds and curtains

Many of us never tire of opening or closing blinds from our bed or sofa, or when we're not at home. With the right fabrics, they can also help heat your home and stop it over-heating.

Find out more

Control systems

Part of our job is to make your home simple to use, so you want to use and enjoy it. For the record, we still believe wholeheartedly in the power of a simple on-off switch.

Find out more

Security systems

What used to be reserved for banks or Fort Knox is now at your fingertips. Modern technology makes it easy to use, which helps make sure it actually is used, for real peace of mind.

How we do it

Design, install, deliver

Three simple stages, with you and your team involved at every one.

  1. 01

    Design

    We help you with the technology design of your home: from wiring, to interiors, to how it's going to work. Collaboration is key. We listen, advise and work with you and your team.

  2. 02

    Installation

    We work with you, your builders and electricians to install. Kind, courteous and considerate. It's your home, and we treat it that way.

  3. 03

    Delivery

    We hand over and walk through everything with you. And we leave you with a clear and simple instruction book in case you forget anything, or something goes wrong.
