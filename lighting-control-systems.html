Lighting control systems

Lighting control: design, installation and support

A lighting control system gives you all the options you need: simple, beautiful light switches and keypads; mood lighting scenes and whole-home controls; and app control wherever you are.

Talk to us 01737 448049
Press a scene

Relax: lamps and wall lights, ceiling down low

Try it

One button, the whole room

How many times a day do you switch the lights on or off? The benefits of a lighting control system become significant over time.

Our lighting control system of choice is Lutron. It's the market leader for many reasons: flexibility, reliability, efficiency and how well it dims all types of lights. With its Connect Bridge you can simply control your home, or update scenes and timers yourself. For smaller systems, Rako also offer great solutions.

  • Lutron
  • Rako

Why lighting control

Create your mood, still with a simple switch

"Just right", every time

Rooms come on at the level that looks right, rather than full brightness. If you like to set and forget, we can do it for the whole house, with one button.

Save energy

By adjusting light levels to match the time of day, we can save significant amounts of energy. You don't need every light at 100% in the evening.

Deter opportunists

Lutron and Rako systems can "replay" activity, bringing certain lights on and off at certain times of day while you're away.

Simple, consistent keypads

Intuitive programming of buttons on well-placed keypads, with the same understandable controls in every room.

Light and health

Better light can mean a healthier home

Modern light bulbs and fixtures can make a vast difference to your health. It's important to consider the quality of the light if you'll be spending long periods of time under it.
