Keeping you, your family and your home safe

CCTV

It's easier than ever to check what's going on, or what's been going on, at your home. For many would-be intruders, it's also an irritating deterrent.

Cameras can now be as small or as big as you like, for discretion or deterrence. Footage is recorded in your home, so you're not relying on the internet to capture it, and you have easy access to live and recorded video from your phone or computer.

We work with you to make sure every area is covered the way you want, using the latest CCTV technology, all in the best available detail and resolution:

  • Clear night vision
  • Motion detection
  • Movement tracking
  • Panoramics
  • Touring

We'll also work with your designers and security teams on how this works with your external lights. Movement detected by the cameras can switch lights on, a further deterrent that also improves the clarity of what's recorded.

And we work with approved, trusted third-party monitoring teams to offer 24/7 support, up to and including monitoring of the cameras when you need it.

Intercom

Ring, Google Nest Doorbell and their competitors have created great experiences on phones, but they still rely on your phone. Take your intercom to the next level with:

  • A beautifully finished entry panel at the gates
  • Dedicated video monitors in the home, as well as the video app on your phone: answer on either
  • The ability to open the gates for visitors
  • A link to your CCTV recorder, so all footage is safely stored for up to a month (longer if you need it), accessible from your phone or computer

We'll work with your designers or gate company so the external panel fits the design of your property. The same goes for the internal handsets: we'll carefully place them so you can answer the door with ease.

Access control

Access control secures your property conveniently. PIN code entry to the main gate, or fob swipes, are all much easier than large bunches of keys. Very often these are built into the intercom, so the entrance panel at the front gate has everything in one: your PIN pad or fob reader, a video camera to see who's arriving, and a speaker and microphone to talk to visitors.

We can set up different PINs, or issue separate fobs, for family members, gardeners, cleaners and so on. Each can be allowed in only at specific times, so you have peace of mind that you know who's allowed into your home, and when. In case you ever need it, it's all logged in the background too.

Looking for an intruder alarm?

We don't fit intruder alarms, but we work with some great companies who do. Get in touch and we can point you in the right direction.
